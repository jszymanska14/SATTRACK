    Index Reference Guide

    🌿 VEGETATION

    NDVI (B8, B4)

    Normalised Difference Vegetation Index = (B8−B4)/(B8+B4). Range −1 to +1. Dense healthy vegetation >0.6. Sparse/stressed <0.4. Bare soil ~0.1.

    🌿 VEGETATION

    NDVI_B8A (B8A, B4)

    Narrower NIR band (865 nm). More sensitive to canopy structure. Preferred for separating vegetation from bright soil.

    💧 WATER

    2BDA (B5/B4)

    Clean water ~0.8–1.0. Algal bloom >1.1. Higher = more chlorophyll.

    💧 WATER

    NDCI & 3BDA

    NDCI: clean ~−0.2–0.0, bloom >0.1. 3BDA corrects for turbidity using B7 — best for inland reservoirs.

    ⚡ AUTO-DETECTION

    Surface type is automatically determined per spectrum using NDVI (B8−B4)/(B8+B4). Threshold: NDVI > 0.20 → Vegetation, otherwise → Water. Bands B4 (665 nm) and B8 (833 nm) are simulated via Gaussian SRF convolution of the ASD spectrum.
